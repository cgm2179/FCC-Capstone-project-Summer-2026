        5G NR band notes

        • Mid-band C-band n77/n78 (3.3–4.2 GHz) and low-band n5/n71 operate dynamically across shifting local raster points (NR-ARFCN global raster), so exact channel numbers vary by market and carrier aggregation config.
        • EARFCN ↔ frequency: fDL = FDL-low + 0.1·(EARFCN − Noffs-DL) MHz; NR-ARFCN uses the 5/15/60 kHz global raster.
        • This viewer now shows every band the scanner picked up (blind scan): 17 LTE E-UTRA bands + 15 NR bands, 619.35–3871.2 MHz. Carrier is decoded from MCC/MNC where SIB1 was read — T-Mobile (310/260) and Verizon (311/480, incl. LTE Band 13 700 MHz, PCI 396/397/398).
